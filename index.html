1. Introduction

Sentinel-AI is an advanced system designed to detect and mitigate zero-day attacks before they compromise the network. Our framework uses deep learning patterns to understand the "behavior" of files rather than just their signatures.

2. Problem Statement

The rise of cross-platform malware (Node.js and Electron) has created a significant gap in traditional security measures. These threats can run on Windows, Mac, and Linux simultaneously, making them extremely dangerous.
