Threat Analysis

1. Denial of Service (DoS) Risks: By emulating high-velocity traffic, we test if the network can handle mass packet injection. Without mitigation, this leads to Kernel Panics and device resets.
2. Packet Sniffing & MitM: Our simulation revealed that unencrypted IoT data is highly vulnerable to sniffing. This allows attackers to capture sensitive credentials in real-time.
