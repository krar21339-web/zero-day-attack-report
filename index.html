03. Introduction

In the modern digital era, cybersecurity has become a critical pillar for protecting sensitive data and infrastructure. As we move into 2026, the rise of sophisticated malware and "Zero-Day" attacks has outpaced traditional defense mechanisms that rely on static signatures. To counter these advanced threats, the integration of Machine Learning and Deep Learning is no longer an option but a necessity. This report aims to explore how these intelligent technologies can analyze behavioral patterns to detect and neutralize unknown threats before they cause significant damage.

The Evolution of Threats

Cybersecurity is not just about blocking known viruses anymore; it's about predicting the unknown behavior of attackers who utilize AI to exploit new vulnerabilities.

04. Zero-Day Attack Overview

A Zero-Day attack refers to a cyber attack that targets a software vulnerability unknown to the software vendor or the public. The term "Zero-Day" signifies that the developer has zero days to fix the issue before it can be exploited...
