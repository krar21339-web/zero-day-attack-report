01. Executive Introduction

This research, conducted at Al-Mamoon University College, explores the critical role of Deep Learning in identifying unknown cyber threats. As attackers develop more sophisticated polymorphic malware, traditional signature-based detection becomes obsolete. Sentinel AI leverages behavioral DNA analysis to monitor system calls in real-time, providing a proactive shield for sensitive infrastructure.

02. Problem Definition

The primary problem addressed in this study is the "Zero-Day Window"— the period between the launch of a new exploit and the release of a security patch. Traditional antivirus software requires a known signature, leaving systems vulnerable. Our engineering challenge is to create a model that can predict malice based on intent, not just identity.

07. Practical Implementation
