Introduction

The Internet of Things (IoT) has become a fundamental pillar in the architecture of smart cities and industrial automation. However, the surge in connected devices introduces significant security vulnerabilities. This project focuses on IoT Traffic Emulation to provide a controlled environment for analyzing network behavior. By utilizing the ESP32, we can simulate realistic data patterns and evaluate how cybersecurity frameworks respond to various traffic loads and potential anomalies in real-time.

Technical Specifications

1. The ESP32 Microcontroller Unit (MCU) The ESP32 is the brain of our emulation system. It is a powerful Dual-Core processor with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth capabilities. In this project, it executes the firmware responsible for generating synthetic TCP/UDP traffic, mimicking multiple IoT sensors simultaneously to test network bandwidth and security protocols.
2. OLED Diagnostic Interface (SSD1306) The 0.96-inch OLED display serves as our hardware debugger. It connects to the MCU via the I2C protocol. Its primary role is to provide real-time visual metrics, displaying the current IP address, the number of packets successfully sent, and any transmission errors detected during the traffic emulation process.
System Wiring & Logic

We utilize Jumper Wires and a Breadboard to establish a stable communication bridge between components. The connectivity follows a strict I2C mapping to ensure high-speed data transfer between the processor and the display.
