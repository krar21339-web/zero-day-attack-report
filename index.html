Section 04

Threat Intelligence

The deployment of ESP32-based IoT nodes introduces significant Attack Vectors. Our technical assessment identifies the following critical vulnerabilities.

1
Resource Exhaustion (DDoS Flooding)

Mass packet injection leads to Kernel Panics and total service blackout of sensor services.

2
Packet Sniffing & MitM Attacks

Intercepting unencrypted frames to expose MQTT credentials and raw telemetry.

Hardening Recommendation

Enable Flash Encryption and Secure Boot V2 to ensure end-to-end data confidentiality.
