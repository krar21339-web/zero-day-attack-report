01. Introduction & Background

In this project, we explore the mechanics of IoT traffic generation and the vulnerabilities associated with interconnected devices. Using the ESP32 as a core emulator, we simulate various traffic conditions to test the robustness of cybersecurity frameworks.

Key Objective: To understand how malicious actors can exploit IoT communication protocols and how to implement hardware-level defenses.
