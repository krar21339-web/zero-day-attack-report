Section 04

Threat Intelligence

The deployment of ESP32-based IoT nodes without rigorous security auditing introduces significant Attack Vectors. Our technical assessment identifies the following critical vulnerabilities.

1
Resource Exhaustion (DDoS Flooding)

By emulating high-velocity SYN/ACK traffic, an attacker can overwhelm the TCP/IP stack, leading to Kernel Panics and total service blackout.

2
Packet Sniffing & MitM Attacks

Unencrypted 802.11 frames are vulnerable to Promiscuous Mode sniffing, exposing raw telemetry and credentials to unauthorized observers.

3
Firmware Dumping

Physical access enables JTAG/UART debugging, allowing attackers to dump Flash Memory and reverse-engineer proprietary logic.

Hardening Recommendation

Enable Flash Encryption and Secure Boot V2. All traffic must be tunneled via TLS 1.3 to ensure end-to-end confidentiality.
